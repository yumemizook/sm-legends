EX-specific scoring calculations:
Note: Both Normal and EX scoring mechanics are tracked at the same time as the player plays the chart.

Timings:
P-Extraordinary: +-10ms
P-Critical: +-16.67ms
Perfect: +-33.33ms
Perfect(Low): +-50ms
Okay(High): +-66.67ms
Okay: +-80ms
Okay(Low): +-100ms
Miss: >100ms  - technically not always a miss on Normal mode, but it is treated as such when EX mode is on.

Scoring (the EX score should be displayed with two decimal points):
P-Extraordinary: 100%
P-Critical: 90%
Perfect: 75%
Perfect(Low): 70%
Okay(High): 40%
Okay: 20%
Okay(Low): 5%
Miss: 0%

Special Grades (displayed seperately from the Normal Grade):
6 Stars: 100%
5 Stars: 95%
4 Stars: 88.5%
3 Stars: 82%
2 Stars: 73%
1 Star: 60%
